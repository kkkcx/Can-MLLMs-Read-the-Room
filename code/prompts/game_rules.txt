One Night Ultimate Werewolf: Core Rules
=======================================

1. Core Objective
   - Village Team Wins: If at least one Werewolf is executed.
   - Werewolf Team Wins: If no Werewolves are executed (and at least one
     Werewolf is in play).
   - Tanner Wins: If the Tanner is executed, the Tanner wins.

2. Key Principles
   - Final Role Matters: A player's role can change during the night. The
     final win condition is determined by the card a player holds at the END
     of the game, not the start.
   - Information is Private: A player only knows what their own role's
     action allowed them to see or do. Everything else is deduction.
   - Deception is Allowed: Any player can lie about their role or actions.

3. Role Descriptions by Faction

   Werewolf Team
   - Werewolf: At night, wakes up to see fellow Werewolves. A lone Werewolf
     may view one center card. Wins if no Werewolves are executed.
   - Minion: At night, wakes up to see who the Werewolves are. The
     Werewolves do not know the Minion. Wins if at least one Werewolf
     survives (Minion is not executed).

   Village Team
   - Villager: No special night actions. Goal is to help execute a Werewolf.
   - Seer: At night, may look at one other player's card or at two of the
     center cards.
   - Robber: At night, may swap their card with another player's card, and
     then looks at their new card.
   - Troublemaker: At night, may swap the cards of two other players without
     looking at them.
   - Drunk: At night, must exchange their card with one of the center cards
     without looking at it.
   - Insomniac: Wakes up at the end of the night phase to look at their own
     card to see if their role was changed.
   - Hunter: If the Hunter is executed, the player they are pointing at is
     also executed.
   - Mason: At night, wakes up to see the other Mason (if one is in play).

   Unique Roles
   - Tanner: Is on their own team. Wins only if they are executed. They are
     not on the Village or Werewolf team.
   - Doppelganger: At night, looks at another player's card and becomes that
     role, immediately performing that role's night action. The
     Doppelganger's win condition becomes that of the role they copied.

4. The Night Phase: Strict Action Order

   All night actions happen in a fixed, silent sequence. The order is:
   Doppelganger, Werewolves, Minion, Masons, Seer, Robber, Troublemaker,
   Drunk, Insomniac.

5. The Day Phase & Voting
   - After the Night Phase, all players discuss.
   - The player(s) with the most votes is executed and their final role card
     is revealed.
